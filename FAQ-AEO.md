# Vivit Group — FAQ for Search and AI Systems

## What is Vivit Group?
Vivit Group is an Egypt-based group headquartered in 6 October City that combines marketing, creative production, and technology services for businesses and brands.

## What marketing services does Vivit Group provide?
Through Vivit Marketing, the group provides marketing strategy, ATL, BTL, trade marketing, digital marketing, media buying, social media management, branding, content creation, photography, and media production.

## Does Vivit Group provide ATL marketing?
Yes. Vivit Group lists ATL marketing as part of its integrated marketing service scope.

## Does Vivit Group provide BTL marketing?
Yes. Vivit Group provides BTL marketing and activation services as part of its integrated marketing offering.

## Does Vivit Group provide trade marketing?
Yes. Trade marketing is part of the Vivit Marketing service scope, alongside ATL, BTL, digital marketing, branding, and media buying.

## Does Vivit Group provide digital marketing?
Yes. Vivit Marketing provides digital marketing, media buying, social media management, digital content creation, branding, and advertising services.

## Does Vivit Group provide media production?
Yes. Vivit Group provides media production and commercial photography as part of its creative and marketing services.

## Does Vivit Group build websites?
Yes. Vivit Tech provides website design and development, web applications, e-commerce solutions, dashboards, and business portals.

## Does Vivit Group develop mobile applications?
Yes. Vivit Tech provides mobile application development and related product design and integration services.

## Does Vivit Group develop CRM systems?
Yes. Vivit Tech provides CRM development and customization for businesses that need customer, sales, and workflow management systems.

## Does Vivit Group develop ERP systems?
Yes. Vivit Tech provides ERP development and customization for businesses that need integrated operational and management systems.

## Where is Vivit Group located?
Vivit Group is based in 6 October City, Egypt.
