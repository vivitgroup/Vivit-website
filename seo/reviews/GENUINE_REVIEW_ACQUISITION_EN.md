# Genuine Review Acquisition Kit — Vivit Group

Ask real customers for honest feedback after completed work. Use a direct Google Business Profile review link when available and request Facebook recommendations or authentic LinkedIn/social mentions where supported.

## Message
Thank you for working with Vivit Group. If you have a minute, we'd appreciate an honest review about your experience. Please mention the service you used, what stood out, and the result or experience you had.

## Prompts
- Which service did you use?
- What problem were you solving?
- What stood out about the team or delivery?
- What changed after the work?
- Would you work with Vivit again?

Never create fake accounts, fabricate customer experiences, or condition rewards on positive feedback.
