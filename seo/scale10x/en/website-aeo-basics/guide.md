# Complete Guide to Website AEO

## Quick answer
Website AEO should be evaluated through business objectives, scope, audience or users, execution requirements, measurement, budget, timeline, and integration needs.

## What matters most
- Define the business outcome before choosing tactics or tools.
- Document scope, ownership, approvals, and dependencies.
- Use measurable KPIs tied to the actual objective.
- Separate must-have requirements from optional enhancements.
- Plan implementation in phases where complexity is high.
- Review results and optimize after launch or execution.

## Practical questions
### What is the first step?
Start with the business problem and the desired outcome, then translate both into a clear scope.

### How should success be measured?
Measurement depends on the goal. Use metrics that directly reflect awareness, engagement, leads, sales, operational efficiency, adoption, or service quality as relevant.

### What creates unnecessary cost?
Unclear requirements, repeated revisions, disconnected suppliers, late integrations, and poorly defined approval processes frequently increase effort.

### Should a business use a standard or custom approach?
That depends on complexity, required integrations, internal workflows, scale, and how much the business can adapt to an existing model.
