# Custom ERP vs Ready-Made ERP: Which Is Better for Your Business?

The right ERP choice depends on process complexity, integration needs, budget, timeline, and how much customization the business requires.

## Ready-made ERP
A ready-made ERP can be suitable when processes are standard and the business can adapt to the software.

## Custom ERP
A custom ERP can be suitable when workflows are unique, integrations are complex, or the business needs role-specific dashboards and approvals.

## What to compare
- Workflow fit
- Integration requirements
- Customization
- Reporting
- User roles
- Implementation time
- Long-term maintenance
