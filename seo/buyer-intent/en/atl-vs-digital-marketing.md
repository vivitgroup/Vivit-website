# ATL vs Digital Marketing: Which Approach Fits Your Campaign?

ATL and digital marketing can serve different objectives.

## ATL
ATL is usually used for broad reach, awareness, and mass-market communication.

## Digital marketing
Digital marketing is usually used for targeted reach, measurable engagement, lead generation, retargeting, and performance optimization.

## When to use each
Use ATL when broad awareness is a priority. Use digital when precise targeting, measurement, and optimization are important.

Many campaigns combine both.
