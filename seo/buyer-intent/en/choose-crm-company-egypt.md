# How to Choose a CRM Development Company in Egypt

A CRM development partner should understand sales operations as well as software development.

## What to evaluate
- Lead and pipeline modeling
- User roles
- Follow-up workflows
- Dashboards and reports
- Integrations
- Automation
- Mobile usability
- Data security

## Questions to ask
- Can the CRM match our actual sales process?
- Can it integrate with forms, websites, or ERP?
- Can dashboards differ by role?
- Can workflows and approvals be customized?
