# How Much Does a Business Website Cost in Egypt?

There is no single fixed price for a business website because scope can vary significantly.

## Main cost factors
- Number of pages
- Custom design vs template
- Arabic and English support
- E-commerce requirements
- Forms and lead capture
- CRM integration
- Custom dashboards or portals
- Content production
- SEO requirements
- Hosting and maintenance

## What to request in a proposal
Ask for a clear scope covering design, development, mobile responsiveness, SEO basics, integrations, testing, launch, and support.
