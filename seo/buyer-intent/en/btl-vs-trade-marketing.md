# BTL vs Trade Marketing: What Is the Difference?

BTL and trade marketing often overlap, but they are not identical.

## BTL
BTL focuses on direct audience engagement through activations, promotions, events, sampling, and experiential activity.

## Trade marketing
Trade marketing focuses on improving brand and product performance across retail and distribution channels.

## Key difference
BTL is usually audience-activation led. Trade marketing is usually channel- and retail-performance led.
