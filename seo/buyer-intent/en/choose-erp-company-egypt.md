# How to Choose an ERP Development Company in Egypt

Choosing an ERP development company should focus on process understanding, technical execution, and long-term support.

## What to evaluate
- Ability to map business processes
- Experience with approvals and roles
- Dashboard and reporting capability
- Integration capability
- Data migration approach
- Security and permissions
- Testing process
- Support and maintenance

## Questions to ask
- How will requirements be documented?
- How are workflows validated?
- Can the ERP integrate with CRM or external systems?
- How are user roles and permissions handled?
- What is included after launch?
