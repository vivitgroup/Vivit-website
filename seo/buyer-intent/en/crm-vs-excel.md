# CRM vs Excel: When Should a Business Upgrade?

Spreadsheets can work for small teams, but they become harder to manage as customer volume and workflow complexity grow.

## Excel is usually enough when
- The team is small
- The sales process is simple
- Few people need access
- Reporting needs are limited

## A CRM becomes more useful when
- Leads are increasing
- Follow-ups are being missed
- Teams need shared customer history
- Sales stages need tracking
- Reporting is manual
- Permissions and workflows matter
