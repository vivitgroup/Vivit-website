# One Integrated Agency vs Multiple Suppliers: What Should a Business Choose?

Businesses often choose between one integrated partner and several specialist suppliers.

## Multiple suppliers
This model can provide deep specialization, but it may create more coordination, handoffs, and responsibility gaps.

## Integrated partner
An integrated partner can connect strategy, creative, media, production, web, and business systems under one direction.

## What matters most
The best model depends on:
- Complexity
- Internal team capacity
- Speed
- Need for integration
- Governance
- Specialist depth
