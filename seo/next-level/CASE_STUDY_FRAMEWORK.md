# Vivit Group — Evidence-First Case Study Framework

Case studies are one of the strongest remaining gaps for SEO, GEO, AEO, and trust.

## Required structure

### 1. Client or industry
Use the client name only with permission. Otherwise describe the industry accurately.

### 2. Business challenge
Explain the real commercial or operational problem.

### 3. Scope
List the actual services delivered.

### 4. Approach
Document strategy, planning, production, development, integrations, or workflows.

### 5. Deliverables
List what was actually produced or implemented.

### 6. Evidence
Use only verified metrics, approved client feedback, screenshots, public links, campaign assets, or system outputs.

### 7. Outcome
Explain the business result without inventing numbers.

### 8. Related services
Link back to relevant Vivit service pages.

## Never publish
- Invented performance statistics
- Fake testimonials
- Unverified client names
- Claims such as "#1", "best", or "leading" without evidence
