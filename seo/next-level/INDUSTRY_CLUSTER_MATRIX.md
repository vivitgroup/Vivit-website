# Vivit Group — Industry Cluster Matrix

Create unique, evidence-led content by industry only where Vivit has a genuine service fit.

## Priority industries

### FMCG
ATL, BTL, trade marketing, retail activation, POSM, merchandising, media production, digital, CRM, dashboards, AI demand/marketing analytics.

### Retail
Trade marketing, shopper marketing, promotions, retail activation, POSM, e-commerce, CRM, loyalty workflows, dashboards, AI recommendations.

### Real Estate
Digital marketing, lead generation, media buying, content production, websites, landing pages, CRM, sales pipelines, dashboards, AI lead scoring.

### Healthcare
Digital marketing, content, websites, appointment/lead workflows, CRM, portals, dashboards, AI support tools subject to appropriate governance.

### Hospitality
Branding, digital marketing, media production, websites, booking/lead workflows, CRM, portals, dashboards, AI guest-support workflows.

### B2B
Brand strategy, lead generation, websites, content, CRM, sales automation, dashboards, AI sales copilots.

### E-commerce
Performance marketing, media buying, content, e-commerce development, CRM, analytics, recommendations, AI customer support.

### Professional Services
Branding, websites, lead generation, CRM, dashboards, workflow automation, AI knowledge assistants.

## Content rule
Every industry page must include:
- Industry-specific problems
- Relevant services
- Distinct workflows
- Industry-specific buyer questions
- Evidence or case-study references where available
- No generic copy-swapping between industries
