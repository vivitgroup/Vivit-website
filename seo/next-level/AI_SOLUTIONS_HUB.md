Vivit Group combines marketing, technology, and AI-oriented business solutions for companies that need practical automation, smarter workflows, and AI-enabled customer and operational experiences.

Automate repetitive business workflows using AI-assisted classification, summarization, routing, document processing, and human-approval flows.

### AI Agents
Design AI agents that can perform defined business tasks using approved tools, APIs, data sources, and controlled workflows.

### AI Chatbots
Build customer-facing or internal AI chatbots connected to approved knowledge bases, CRM systems, websites, and support workflows.

### RAG Solutions
Create retrieval-augmented generation systems that answer questions using approved company documents and controlled knowledge sources.

### AI for CRM
Use AI for lead scoring, summaries, next-best-action recommendations, follow-up assistance, pipeline support, and reporting.

### AI for ERP
Use AI for forecasting, anomaly detection, document processing, operational assistants, and decision support.

### AI for Marketing
Apply AI to segmentation, creative ideation, campaign analysis, reporting, customer journeys, lead scoring, and content workflows.

### AI Analytics
Enable natural-language analytics, anomaly detection, forecasting, insight generation, and business intelligence support.

### AI Governance
Define privacy, access controls, evaluation, human oversight, auditability, and operational guardrails.
