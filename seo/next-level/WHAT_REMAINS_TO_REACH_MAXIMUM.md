# Vivit Group — What Remains to Reach Maximum Search Visibility

## Current foundation
Strong entity framework, bilingual service architecture, topic authority, buyer-intent content, AI solution clusters, metadata templates, schema templates, keyword universes, and review-acquisition framework.

## Major remaining gaps

### 1. Main-domain publishing
Move the highest-value content onto vivit-group.com with canonical URLs.

### 2. Technical SEO
Control titles, meta descriptions, H1/H2, canonical, hreflang, robots, sitemap, structured data, redirects, Core Web Vitals, rendering, and internal linking.

### 3. Google Search Console
Track impressions, clicks, queries, indexing, page coverage, and ranking movement.

### 4. Google Business Profile
Complete categories, services, description, photos, posts, Q&A, reviews, and responses.

### 5. Real case studies
Publish evidence-backed work for Marketing, ATL, BTL, Trade Marketing, Digital, Production, Web, Apps, CRM, ERP, and AI.

### 6. Backlinks and mentions
Earn relevant links and mentions from clients, partners, publications, directories, associations, event pages, and industry sites.

### 7. AI citations
Increase public, consistent, evidence-rich references that AI systems can retrieve and cite.

### 8. Review volume
Acquire authentic reviews steadily from real customers.

### 9. Continuous measurement
Track query families and AI answer prompts monthly, consolidate cannibalizing pages, and update high-value pages.

## Practical ceiling
No legitimate process can guarantee first position for every query or every AI answer. The objective is maximum discoverability, authority, consistency, and evidence across the search ecosystem.
