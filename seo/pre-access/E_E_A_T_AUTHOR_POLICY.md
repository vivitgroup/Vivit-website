# E-E-A-T / Author & Editorial Policy

## Company content
Every expert article should identify Vivit Group or a real named contributor where appropriate.

## Author profiles
Use only real team members.
Include factual role, experience areas, and public professional links.
Do not invent credentials.

## Review process
Marketing topics: reviewed by a qualified Vivit Marketing team member.
Tech/AI topics: reviewed by a qualified Vivit Tech team member.
Legal, medical, financial or regulated claims require appropriate specialist review.

## Evidence
Prefer real project evidence, screenshots, public links, approved client feedback, and measurable results when available.

## Updates
Display meaningful update dates only when content materially changes.
