# Vivit Group — ICP & Buyer Persona Matrix

CEO/Founder: growth, positioning, integrated supplier.
CMO/Marketing Director: campaigns, media, brand, reporting.
Sales Director: leads, CRM, pipeline, automation.
COO/Operations: ERP, workflows, approvals, dashboards.
CTO/IT: architecture, integration, security, maintainability.
Procurement: scope, risk, comparison, deliverables.
Brand/Trade Manager: activation, retail execution, POSM, merchandising.

Every commercial page should answer who the service is for, what problem it solves, required inputs, deliverables, KPIs, buying questions, and next step.
