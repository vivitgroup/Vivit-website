# AI / Search Crawler Accessibility Checklist

## Before launch
- Critical content is server-rendered or reliably crawlable
- robots.txt does not unintentionally block public content
- No blanket noindex on service/article templates
- Canonicals resolve correctly
- Sitemaps list canonical pages
- Public assets needed for rendering are crawlable
- Important text is not available only after interaction
- Structured data matches visible content

## AI discoverability
Keep entity facts clear and consistent.
Use direct, self-contained answers.
Publish evidence-backed case studies.
Use stable public URLs.
Avoid hiding core information behind scripts, login or forms.

## Rule
Do not add crawler-specific claims or fake machine-only content. Content for AI systems must also be useful to human visitors.
