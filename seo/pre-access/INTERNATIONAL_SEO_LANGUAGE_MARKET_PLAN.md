# Vivit Group — International SEO & Multilingual Market Plan

## Priority languages
Arabic, English, German, French, Spanish, Polish, Swedish, Chinese.

## Recommended URL model
/en/ /ar/ /de/ /fr/ /es/ /pl/ /sv/ /zh/

## Rules
- One stable URL per language.
- hreflang for true equivalents.
- Natural localization, not literal machine translation.
- Translate descriptions, not the company name.
- Do not create country pages without real market relevance.
- Prioritize homepage, hubs, money pages, case studies, then authority content.
