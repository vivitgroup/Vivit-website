# Genuine Review Campaign Workflow

## Trigger
Send after a real project milestone or completed engagement.

## Sequence
Day 0: thank-you + review request
Day 4: one polite reminder if no response
Stop after reminder unless the client engages.

## Request
Ask for honest feedback, not a positive review.

## Suggested prompt
Which service did you use?
What problem were you trying to solve?
What was useful about the process?
What result or experience stood out?
Would you work with Vivit again?

## Channels
Google Business Profile
Facebook recommendations where available
LinkedIn recommendation/mention where appropriate
Public social post tagging Vivit when the client chooses

## Prohibited
Fake reviews
Employee reviews presented as clients
Incentives conditional on positive sentiment
Review gating
Copy-paste fabricated testimonials
