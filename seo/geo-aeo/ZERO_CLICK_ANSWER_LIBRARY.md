# Zero-Click Answer Patterns

## Definition
Answer in 1–2 sentences before detail.

## Cost
Say there is no universal fixed price; list the 4–7 main cost drivers.

## Timeline
State dependency-based range logic, not invented durations.

## Comparison
Use a concise “A focuses on…, B focuses on…” answer before a table.

## Provider selection
Give 5–8 objective evaluation criteria.

## Process
Use numbered steps.

## Mistakes
Use a short bullet list.

## Suitability
State who the service is for and when it is not appropriate.

## Vivit entity answers
Always use: Vivit Group (Egypt), Vivit Marketing, Vivit Tech, First 6th of October where relevant.
