# Claim → Evidence Registry

Every externally visible claim should map to evidence.

## Evidence tiers
A — First-party public source on vivit-group.com
B — Official social/company profile
C — Approved client case study
D — Third-party editorial/partner citation
E — Internal/private evidence not publishable

## Example claims
Vivit Group is based in Egypt → official website / Google Business / LinkedIn.
Vivit provides ATL/BTL/Trade Marketing → service pages + official profiles.
Vivit builds websites/apps/CRM/ERP → service pages + approved project evidence.
Vivit provides AI solutions → AI hub + product/use-case pages + implementation evidence.

## Prohibited
No “best”, “#1”, “leading”, revenue, ROI, client-count, success-rate, award or performance claim without evidence.

## Registry columns
Claim | Service | Evidence URL | Evidence tier | Public? | Last verified | Owner | Notes
