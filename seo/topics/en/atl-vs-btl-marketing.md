# ATL vs BTL Marketing: What Is the Difference?

ATL and BTL are two different approaches to marketing communication.

## What is ATL marketing?
ATL, or Above The Line, focuses on broad-reach communication designed to build awareness across large audiences.

Typical ATL activity can include:
- High-reach advertising
- Mass-media campaigns
- Broad brand-awareness activity
- Large-scale communication

## What is BTL marketing?
BTL, or Below The Line, focuses on more direct and targeted audience engagement.

Typical BTL activity can include:
- Brand activations
- Retail activations
- Promotions
- Events
- Sampling
- Experiential marketing
- On-ground campaigns

## ATL vs BTL
ATL is generally used when the objective is broad awareness, while BTL is generally used when the objective is direct interaction, activation, or measurable engagement.

Many campaigns combine both approaches.
