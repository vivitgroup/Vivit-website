# When Does a Business Need a Custom CRM?

A custom CRM can make sense when generic tools do not match a company's sales process or operational workflow.

## Signs you may need a custom CRM
- Your sales pipeline has unique stages
- Teams rely heavily on spreadsheets
- Customer data is fragmented
- Reporting is manual
- Approvals are complex
- Existing systems do not integrate
- You need role-based dashboards
- You need workflow automation

## What a custom CRM can include
Depending on requirements, a CRM can include:
- Lead management
- Customer records
- Sales pipeline
- Tasks and follow-ups
- Approvals
- Dashboards
- Reports
- Integrations
- Role-based access
