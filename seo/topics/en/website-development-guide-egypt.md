# What Should a Business Website Include in 2026?

A modern business website should do more than present information. It should support discovery, trust, conversion, and integration with business systems.

## Core elements
A strong website should typically include:
- Clear service architecture
- Fast mobile experience
- Search-friendly content
- Strong calls to action
- Contact and lead capture
- Case studies or proof
- Structured navigation
- Analytics and measurement
- CRM or workflow integration where needed

## SEO and AEO readiness
Modern websites should use clear page structure, descriptive titles, structured data where appropriate, and direct answers to important customer questions.
