# CRM vs ERP: What Is the Difference?

CRM and ERP solve different business problems, although they can work together.

## What is CRM?
CRM, or Customer Relationship Management, focuses on customer data, leads, sales pipelines, communication, follow-up, and customer-facing workflows.

## What is ERP?
ERP, or Enterprise Resource Planning, focuses on integrated operational processes across departments such as finance, operations, approvals, inventory, procurement, and management reporting.

## CRM vs ERP
CRM is usually centered around customers and revenue workflows. ERP is usually centered around internal operations and cross-department processes.

## Can CRM and ERP integrate?
Yes. Integration can reduce duplicate data entry and help customer, sales, finance, and operations teams work from connected information.
