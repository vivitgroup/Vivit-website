# Trade Marketing in Egypt: A Practical Guide

Trade marketing focuses on improving how products perform across distribution and retail channels.

## What does trade marketing include?
Common areas include:
- Retail visibility
- Merchandising
- POSM
- Promotions
- In-store activation
- Channel campaigns
- Retailer support
- Sales enablement materials

## Trade marketing vs consumer marketing
Consumer marketing focuses primarily on end customers. Trade marketing focuses on the route to market, retail environment, distributors, retailers, and channel execution.

## Why trade marketing matters
Strong trade marketing can help a brand improve visibility at the point of sale, support sales teams, strengthen retailer relationships, and execute promotions more consistently.
