# Retail Activation, POSM and Merchandising Explained

Retail marketing often depends on three practical execution areas: activation, point-of-sale materials, and merchandising.

## Retail activation
Retail activation is designed to create engagement inside or around a retail environment. It can include promotions, demonstrations, sampling, or branded experiences.

## POSM
POSM stands for Point of Sale Materials. These are branded physical or visual materials used to improve visibility and communication at the point of purchase.

## Merchandising
Merchandising focuses on how products are presented, organized, and made visible in retail environments.

## How they work together
A trade marketing campaign can combine retail activation, POSM, and merchandising to create stronger in-store visibility and customer engagement.
