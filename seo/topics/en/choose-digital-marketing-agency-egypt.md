# How to Choose a Digital Marketing Agency in Egypt

Choosing a digital marketing agency should be based on fit, capability, evidence, and execution—not only on follower counts or presentation quality.

## What to evaluate
Look at:
- Strategy capability
- Creative quality
- Media buying experience
- Content production
- Reporting and measurement
- Website and landing-page capability
- Ability to connect marketing with technology
- Relevant case studies

## Questions to ask
- Who owns the strategy?
- How are campaigns measured?
- Who creates the content?
- Is media buying handled in-house?
- Can the agency build or optimize landing pages?
- Can it integrate CRM or lead-management workflows?
