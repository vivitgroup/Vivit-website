# What Is a Campaign Key Visual?

## Overview
Media Production is a practical business capability that works best when objectives, scope, execution, and measurement are clearly defined.

## What to evaluate
- Business objective
- Required deliverables
- Audience or users
- Timeline and budget
- Measurement and reporting
- Dependencies and integrations
- Ownership, approvals, and quality control

## Practical framework
1. Define the problem and desired outcome.
2. Document scope and constraints.
3. Select measurable KPIs.
4. Build a phased execution plan.
5. Test, review, and optimize.

## Common mistakes
Avoid choosing tactics or software before requirements are clear. Avoid fragmented ownership and reporting that is not tied to business outcomes.
