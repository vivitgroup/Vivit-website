# E-commerce Development in Egypt

## Executive answer
E-commerce Development in Egypt should be approached as a business capability, not as an isolated tool. The strongest implementations connect a clear objective, defined users, measurable outcomes, operational ownership, and the systems or channels required to deliver the result.

## What the scope should cover
- catalog
- checkout
- payments
- inventory
- analytics

## Recommended approach
1. **Define the business problem.** Start with the outcome the business wants, not the technology or channel.
2. **Map the current process.** Identify users, handoffs, data sources, bottlenecks, and existing tools.
3. **Define success metrics.** Choose KPIs that reflect the real objective rather than vanity metrics.
4. **Design the target workflow.** Clarify what should happen, who owns each step, and where automation is appropriate.
5. **Build or execute in phases.** Start with the highest-value scope and reduce unnecessary complexity.
6. **Measure and improve.** Review quality, adoption, conversion, efficiency, or business impact depending on the service.

## Questions buyers should ask
- What exactly is included in the scope?
- Which parts are strategy, production, development, integration, or ongoing management?
- Who owns approvals and changes?
- What data, systems, creative assets, or access are required?
- How will performance be reported?
- What happens after launch or campaign completion?
- Which assumptions could affect cost or timeline?

## Common implementation risks
Unclear requirements, disconnected suppliers, late stakeholder feedback, missing integrations, weak measurement, and unsupported claims can reduce the value of the project. A practical scope should document dependencies before work begins.
